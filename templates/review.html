MSCS 2101 · GROUP G4 · OCTOBER 24, 2026

A clearer path from campus problem to completed repair.

FixTrack brings the report, the assigned worker, the progress, and the resolution into one shared system.

Preliminary Design Review · Initial prototype
01 / THE PROBLEM

Reports lose visibility

Email and scattered messages make it hard to see who owns a repair or whether it has been completed.

02 / THE PROPOSAL

One traceable request

Students report issues. Administrators assign work. Maintenance workers record progress and the final fix.

03 / THE VALUE

Clear responsibility

A focused campus workflow with role-specific access and a visible change history. We are simplifying a known problem, not claiming maintenance software is new.

INITIAL ARCHITECTURE

Simple enough to build and explain

We chose a single Django application to keep authentication, forms, and workflow checks together. SQLite keeps the classroom setup small; production scale would require a separate deployment review.

RISK AND REVIEW QUESTIONS

Agree on the rules before adding more features

Our main delivery risk is inconsistent access or status behavior across roles. Centralized server rules and direct permission tests reduce that risk.

  • Should resolved requests remain closed, or should a later version support reopening?
  • Should requesters be allowed to edit a report before a worker is assigned?
  • Does this limited scope give us enough time to test the complete workflow?

Next: incorporate peer feedback, revise the SRS, expand quality measurements, and review deployment security. Photos, email, single sign-on, and university integrations remain outside this prototype.
